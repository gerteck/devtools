# Devtools Roadmap & Future Enhancements

Proposed utilities and enhancements for future development cycles.

---

## 🚀 Upcoming Utilities

1. **JWT Debugger & Inspector (`/#/jwt`):**
   - Header, Payload, and Signature decoder.
   - Real-time expiration checking and claims formatting.
   - Secret verification with HMAC-SHA256 client-side.

2. **Regex Sandbox & Tester (`/#/regex`):**
   - Live regex matcher with flags (g, i, m, s, u).
   - Match highlight overlay and capture group breakdown table.
   - Common regex cheat sheet and presets.

3. **Base64 / URL / Hash Converter (`/#/crypto`):**
   - Text <-> Base64 / Hex encoder and decoder.
   - Client-side WebCrypto SHA-256 / SHA-512 / MD5 hashing.
   - File drag-and-drop to Base64 data URL.

4. **Timestamp & Cron Expression Parser (`/#/cron`):**
   - Epoch timestamp <-> ISO 8601 / Human readable converter.
   - 5-field cron syntax explainer and schedule preview.

---
